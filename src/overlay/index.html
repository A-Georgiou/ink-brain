Set up Ink Brain

Ink Brain Helper needs a few macOS permissions. Nothing leaves your Mac until you ask Ink Brain something.

  • Screen RecordingSo Ink Brain can see what you point at.
  • MicrophoneSo you can talk to Ink Brain.
  • Speech RecognitionTranscribes your voice on-device.
  • Accessibility (optional)Lets Ink Brain click and type for you, only after you approve each time.

After turning on Screen Recording in System Settings, restart the helper.

Ink Brain reads the page in the background, signed out, and only sees its text and links.

It will see a screenshot whenever it asks to look, and can send its answers to your screen and speakers.

    You will see Ink Brain's cursor. Press Esc or move your mouse to stop it at any time.
